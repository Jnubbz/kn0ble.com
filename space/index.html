Reading this

  • Point at (az) is the compass bearing (true north). Elevation is degrees above the horizon. The circle plot is the sky: center is straight up, edge is the horizon, green dot is where it rises, red is where it sets.
  • Frequencies are Doppler-corrected for the moment shown. FM birds: program the rise / highest / set columns as memories and step through them. Most people fix the 2 m side and only tune the 70 cm side.
  • Orbit data refreshes from CelesTrak every couple of hours (the site caches it) and frequency data from SatNOGS daily. Leave the page open and it keeps itself current. Sync now pulls again and recalculates.
  • Being listed does not mean a repeater is switched on. Check AMSAT live status before a pass.
